Data notes

This public view shows aggregate statistics only. Faculty names are normalized into bilingual combined labels. Rank recognizes Prof/Professor and Hebrew פרופ variants, plus Dr and Hebrew ד"ר variants. Publisher and access model are checked online with OpenAlex Sources metadata. Non-OA journals are labeled Hybrid / Non-OA because source metadata does not prove journal-level hybrid policy for every title.
